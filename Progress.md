- 2026-10-09 M3: gravitational lensing.
  - Pipeline (`src/render/Renderer.ts`): RenderPass (layer 0: stars, prey) > `LensingPass` > bloom > `CorePass` > output. Both composer buffers carry a `DepthTexture`.
  - `src/render/LensingPass.ts` + `src/shaders/lensing.{vert,frag}.glsl`: full-screen thin point-mass lens (deflection E * (E/r)^falloff, smooth window to zero at `radius`). Writes the bent colour AND the bent depth (`gl_FragDepth`), then draws the hole's own parts on top (`CORE_LAYER` core, `OVERLAY_LAYER` disk and photon ring), depth-tested against it. So the core is never bent and CorePass still finds a valid depth after the swap. Centre and core radius come from projecting `player.position` and `player.radius` each frame (exposed as `lensing.centre`, `lensing.coreRadius`).
  - Two layers: the far background (stars) bends with `einsteinRadius` 4; play-plane things (prey, disk) bend with `planeEinsteinRadius` 1.6, scaled by how far behind the hole they are (smoothstep from `planeDepthSoft` in front to `planeDepthRange` behind). Prey in front of the hole do not bend; prey behind it arc around.
  - Scene alpha is the layer mask: clear alpha 0, prey write 1, the starfield uses CustomBlending so it never writes alpha. Filtered alpha also stops bilinear background samples from picking up prey edges.
  - The disk bends itself in its vertex shader (`src/shaders/lib/lensVertex.glsl`, forward map with Newton steps) with the same lens (`lib/lens.glsl`, shared uniforms in `src/render/lensUniforms.ts`). Result: the near side hugs the core, the far side lifts and opens a dark crescent above it.
  - `src/render/layers.ts` holds `CORE_LAYER` (1) and `OVERLAY_LAYER` (2). The core is no longer on layer 0.
  - Quality: `lensing.setSamples(n)` (0 off, 1 tap, up to 4 taps inside `sampleZone` x E; set per quality level since M8). Off also stops the disk bending.
  - `STARFIELD.count` 2500 to 9000 so the lens has enough stars to visibly bend.
  - Smoke test 3: core pixel black with the lens on and off, far side of the disk dark only when lensed, near side bright in both, still clean after a resize. Uses `__spaghettify.readPixel` (reads the frame right after it renders) and `renderer.lensing`.
  - Judged by screenshot (SwiftShader): arcs and dashes around the hole, a full Einstein ring when a star sits behind it, prey behind the hole lifted, big prey in front still cover the core. Perf (GTX 1070 Ti, 3840x2160 render): whole frame about 2.4 ms at quality 0 or 1, 2.6 ms at quality 2. No D3D shader warnings.

A life ends at an absolute `evaporateMass` (half the start mass), so only a tiny starving hole evaporates; a hit never takes a hole below its start mass, so it never ends a life (`forgiveHit` removed).
  - Milestones replace tiers (`MILESTONES`, `src/sim/milestones.ts`): each is the size at which a class becomes food (its largest member edible): Dust, Rock, Boulder, Asteroid, Dwarf planet, Moon, Planet, Ice giant and Gas giant eater. A `milestone` event (once per life, monotonic) plays the banner (`MilestoneBanner`: "NOW BIG ENOUGH TO EAT A ROCK" over "Rock eater"), flash, punch (`MilestoneSetPiece`), shake and sound (`AudioEngine.milestone`, `AUDIO.milestone`). Nothing in the arena changes and nothing slows down: the tier-up slow motion (`World.timeScale`) is removed.
  - Comets: each hole in play has its own timer (12 s first, then 9 to 16 s) and draws a comet just outside its view, sized relative to it (0.6 to 3 radii, clamped to 0.6 to 20); recycled once far from every hole and moving away from all, or out of the arena. Debris lives out its lifetime or leaves the arena.
  - Score: peak mass of the life over the start mass x 1000 (`Hole.score`, `peakMass`). Leaderboard caps (`rules.ts`, `LEADERBOARD`): the peak is at most the start mass doubled every `doublingSeconds` (1) and at most `maxPeakMass` (1e6, about 3 x the arena); at least the milestone's mass; meals per second as before. The submission keeps its `tier` field (now the milestone number, 1 to 9). The Docker api stage now copies `src/sim` (rules imports the milestone masses). Analytics score bands go up to 1e9.
  - Rendering only what is in view: `PreyMesh` is stateless (begin, add, end; colour, spin and stretch from the body, its seed and the time), so instances are refilled every frame from the visible set. `PreyFieldMesh` draws falling bodies, then bodies from the grid, then grain layers biggest first, culled to the camera's ground trapezoid (`GameCamera.groundView`, `inView`) and to `PREY.drawMinPixels` (1); four meshes: rocks at detail 0, 1 and 2 by pixel size (3 and 10 px) and spheres, with budgets 4000, 1500, 600 and 400. Real GPU (GTX 1070 Ti, 1280x720, High): CPU about 1 to 2.6 ms a frame, GPU 3 to 5 ms; at most about 2.5k tiny rocks drawn.
  - `SPAGHETTI.onsetGap` 2.5 to 1.6 hole radii: with the continuous zoom a big hole had most of the screen in the stretch zone and every planet looked like an egg.
  - Debug handle: `milestones` (index, all, masses) replaces `tiers` and `allTiers`; `field` (the MatterField); `prey.edible(radius, gap)` turns an arena body slot into a rock next to the hole, `prey.clear()` sends bodies away and clears the field around the hole, `prey.instanceOf(i)` and `prey.counts` replace `meshOf`; `comets.spawn()` and `reset()` take no tier.
