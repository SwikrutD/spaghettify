- 2026-10-09 M16: mixed arena (awaiting user playtest).
  - One bounded disc (`ARENA.radius` 1200, `src/config/arena.ts`) where every size coexists. Small matter is a procedural field (`src/sim/MatterField.ts`, `FIELD.layers`): dust, pebble, rock, boulder, asteroid and small moon layers, each tiling the arena with square cells of up to grid x grid slots (no overlaps). A grain's place, size and look are a hash of the world seed, layer, cell, slot and the cell's epoch (`hash4` in `rng.ts`), so the sim stores only per-cell bit masks (taken, covered) and regrow times (about 4 MB per world). About 670k dust grains, 134k pebbles, 24k rocks, 7.6k boulders, 1.25k asteroids and 300 small moons; all arena matter is about 356k mass. Dwarf planets, moons, rocky planets, ice giants and gas giants are individual bodies with a fixed population per class (`BODIES`: 160, 70, 18, 6, 3), static, pushed apart if they overlap, placed biggest first.
  - Spatial grid (`src/sim/grid.ts`, `SpatialGrid`): levels of square cells (`ARENA.gridLevels` 64 and 512); a body goes into the first level whose cell is at least twice its radius; queries take `reach + k * radius` (k = 1 overlap, eatReach for eating, bodyPad for contact, pullReach for pull). Rebuilt at the start of every step and after the prey update. Eating, contacts, pull and separation go through it; grains are visited by cell (`field.forEach`). Comets (pool 16) and debris (pool 120) are scanned linearly.
  - Eating: any hole eats the edible bodies and grains it touches (`World.feed`). An eaten grain is taken from the field and a pooled stand-in body (`field.infall`, 96) carries the spaghettification with the grain's look. Grains below `PREY.minRelSize` of a hole are specks to it: swallowed on the way (mass, no meal, no event, no hunger reset). Grains a hole cannot eat block it and pull it like bodies.
  - Respawn: an eaten body respawns as its class somewhere in the arena, out of sight of every hole (`ARENA.holeClearViews` 1.4 view distances) and out of pull reach, clear of other bodies (24 random tries). With no such place (a hole that sees the whole arena) it waits as `gone` and retries every 2 s. Eaten grain cells grow back laid out afresh after 20 to 40 s, only once every hole is 1.8 view distances away. Bodies cover the grains under them; when a body leaves, its patch grows back in time.
  - Edge: a dense gas wall. A hole within its soft width (0.6 of its view distance, at most 300) of the edge is pushed back, growing with the square of the depth to 1.6 x its steering authority at the edge (`src/sim/arena.ts`), with a hard limit 0.3 soft widths out. Drawn by `src/scene/ArenaWall.ts` and `shaders/arenaWall.*.glsl`: a flat annulus on OVERLAY_LAYER with a premultiplied blend (glows and hides, never writes the lens alpha), noise scaled to the viewer's view distance and cross-faded between powers of two so growing never pops.
  - Speed and view from the radius alone: `viewDistance(radius)` and `topSpeed(radius)` in `Hole.ts`; the sim `View` is gone and the camera keeps the smoothing and the portrait pull-back (`GameCamera.aspectScale`). Zoom is continuous (`CAMERA.zoomExponent` 0.8, no tier scale).
  - Growth: `GROWTH.radiusExponent` 0.4 (was 0.5): a hole's mass grows faster than its area, so the same relative meal is worth less the bigger it is. `holeMassForRadius` is the hole curve; prey keep mass = radius squared. A scripted greedy pilot (sim only, perfect targeting, clumsy at avoiding) reached asteroid eater in 30 to 90 s, moon eater in 1 to 2 min and planet eater in 3.5 to 5 min, then swung around radius 60 to 90 as decay and a depleted arena caught up. With 0.5 it ate the whole arena in 30 s.
  - Hunger (`Hunger.ts`): size-scaled Hawking decay, always on for holes of radius 2 and up (`HUNGER.hawkingRate` 1 percent a second at radius 50, linear in radius), plus the old starvation drain (9 percent a second after 7 s without a meal). A life ends at an absolute `evaporateMass` (half the start mass), so only a tiny starving hole evaporates; a hit never takes a hole below its start mass, so it never ends a life (`forgiveHit` removed).
  - Milestones replace tiers (`MILESTONES`, `src/sim/milestones.ts`): each is the size at which a class becomes food (its largest member edible): Dust, Rock, Boulder, Asteroid, Dwarf planet, Moon, Planet, Ice giant and Gas giant eater. A `milestone` event (once per life, monotonic) plays the banner (`MilestoneBanner`: "NOW BIG ENOUGH TO EAT A ROCK" over "Rock eater"), flash, punch (`MilestoneSetPiece`), shake and sound (`AudioEngine.milestone`, `AUDIO.milestone`). Nothing in the arena changes and nothing slows down: the tier-up slow motion (`World.timeScale`) is removed.
  - Comets: each hole in play has its own timer (12 s first, then 9 to 16 s) and draws a comet just outside its view, sized relative to it (0.6 to 3 radii, clamped to 0.6 to 20); recycled once far from every hole and moving away from all, or out of the arena. Debris lives out its lifetime or leaves the arena.
  - Score: peak mass of the life over the start mass x 1000 (`Hole.score`, `peakMass`). Leaderboard caps (`rules.ts`, `LEADERBOARD`): the peak is at most the start mass doubled every `doublingSeconds` (1) and at most `maxPeakMass` (1e6, about 3 x the arena); at least the milestone's mass; meals per second as before. The submission keeps its `tier` field (now the milestone number, 1 to 9). The Docker api stage now copies `src/sim` (rules imports the milestone masses). Analytics score bands go up to 1e9.
  - Rendering only what is in view: `PreyMesh` is stateless (begin, add, end; colour, spin and stretch from the body, its seed and the time), so instances are refilled every frame from the visible set. `PreyFieldMesh` draws falling bodies, then bodies from the grid, then grain layers biggest first, culled to the camera's ground trapezoid (`GameCamera.groundView`, `inView`) and to `PREY.drawMinPixels` (1); four meshes: rocks at detail 0, 1 and 2 by pixel size (3 and 10 px) and spheres, with budgets 4000, 1500, 600 and 400. Real GPU (GTX 1070 Ti, 1280x720, High): CPU about 1 to 2.6 ms a frame, GPU 3 to 5 ms; at most about 2.5k tiny rocks drawn.

- 2026-10-10 M17: bot holes and hole vs hole, offline arena (awaiting a human playtest).
  - Hole vs hole (`World.holesMeet`, `HOLES` in `src/config/holes.ts`): a hole at least `eatRatio` 1.15 times another's radius swallows it once the victim's centre is within `coverFraction` 0.9 of its radius. The eater gains `massTransfer` 0.8 of the victim's mass (its mass is on the hole curve already, so a radius 3.5 hole is far more than 3.5 squared), counted as a meal (disk heat, hunger clock, `biggestEaten` is the hole, flagged `hole`); the rest is radiated away. The victim enters the new phase `eaten` (`Hunger.beEaten`, `Hole.eatOffset`, `eatProgress`), spaghettifies into the eater for `infallTime` 1.2 s, then its life is over: one `died` event now carries `cause` (`evaporated` or `eaten`), `by` and `byName`. A `holeEaten` event drives the feed, ripple, shake, boom and flash. Holes closer in size than the ratio bump when their cores overlap (`bumpOverlap` 0.9): pushed apart, knocked back, stunned, each sheds `DANGER.shedFraction` of its mass as debris (event `bumped` with `by: 'hole'`). A hole in spawn protection (`protection`, 3 s after a respawn, disk blinks) neither eats nor is eaten. `Hole.absent` (eaten or over) holes are out of every rule loop.
  - Bots (`src/sim/Bots.ts`, `BOTS` in config): a per-bot character drawn at each spawn (aggression, caution, reaction 0.18 to 0.55 s, steering wobble, throttle 0.82 to 1), a decision every reaction time (jittered): flee bigger holes (range grows with the other's radius and speed and the bot's caution), hunt smaller ones (range from aggression, lead the target, only if worth a share of its mass or close), else steer for the best meal in sight (mass over squared distance; bodies, grains of the edible non-speck layers, debris, comets; meals beside something inedible are skipped), else wander (pulled back toward the middle when far out); steer around bodies it cannot eat (a path-ahead test, bending to the side it was heading) and away from the gas wall. They only write `hole.input`. `World({ bots })` and `world.botTarget` keep the arena at the target: the missing ones are added at once (a fresh arena starts them at radius 0.5 to 9 so it looks lived in, unprotected), a bot whose life ends comes back after 2 to 6 s as a new character with a new name at radius 0.5 to 2.5. Bots draw no comets. `?bots=N` and `?seed=N` in the URL set the count (default `BOTS.count` 11) and the seed (the Playwright tests use `?bots=0`).
  - Spawning (`World.spawnPoint`, `World.respawn(hole, radius)`): random tries (`ARENA.spawnTries` 60) for a place out of every other hole's sight (`holeClearViews` of theirs plus its own), pulled by bodies it cannot eat by at most `spawnMaxPull` 0.02 of its steering authority and none within `spawnClearViews` of touching (the giants' pull reach covers most of the arena, so "no body in pull reach" had to become "faint pull": `bodyPull` is now shared with Dangers), with `spawnFood` 12 meals in sight; failing that, the best try. Then the small matter that could pull it is cleared as before. Play again in the client is `World.respawn(local)` in the running arena; `World.reset()` (quit to title, tests) starts a whole new arena.
  - Names (`src/sim/names.ts`, `HOLE_NAMES`): "Silent Quasar" style or a real star or physicist with a number ("Kerr-7"), unique among the live holes. The player gets one until they type a nickname.
  - Speed and agility: `PLAYER.speedExponent` 1 to 0.85 (world speed follows the view distance a bit more slowly than the zoom, so a bigger hole is only slightly faster than one a little smaller) and a new `PLAYER.agilityExponent` 0.15 (`responsiveness(radius)`: bigger holes turn and accelerate more slowly). A headless chase (chaser always aims at the fleer) shows a faster hole catches one in the open within 2 to 25 s whatever the curve: escaping a bigger hole is about distance, awareness, cover and the gas wall, not a straight race.
  - Rendering: `HoleMeshes` (`src/player/HoleMeshes.ts`) draws every hole: the local `HoleMesh` plus one per other hole in view (made on first sight, recycled when it leaves the world), all sharing one `DiskTurbulence` map (the per-frame polar noise moved out of `AccretionDisk`), each with its own temperature scale (0.8 to 1.25) and disk angle. A swallowed hole is drawn spiralling into the eater, stretched along the line to it and shrinking (`HoleMesh.update` with the eater's drawn place). The lens: `LensingPass.extras` adds the deflections of up to two other holes (the biggest on screen, `LENSING.extraLenses`, `extraMinCore`) to the main lens in the same shader pass (a uniform array; `planeBehind` and the flipped-image test per lens); the disks of holes that are not lensed stay flat (`AccretionDisk.setLensed`). The prey shader's hole centre is a per-instance attribute now (`aHole`, replaces `uHole`): bodies stretch toward the nearest hole in view that can swallow them (`BodyFrame.holes`, filled by `HoleMeshes.drawn`) and a body falling into any hole winds around that hole. `MergerRipple` (a pooled flat quad with `mergerRipple.*.glsl`) is the gravitational wave ripple.

- 2026-09-28 to 2026-10-09 (single-player MVP, condensed): Project named Spaghettify, self-hosted on TrueNAS. Gameplay plane is XZ, the camera looks toward -Z tilted 58 degrees (screen up is -Z). Mouse follow uses the screen-space offset from the screen centre, so every input source is just a 0..1 vector. The core is redrawn after bloom (CorePass) instead of fighting bloom settings. The hole's own parts are kept out of the lensed image (a lensed core magnifies the shadow into a big black ring); lensing scales with depth behind the hole, stars get the strong lens and the play plane a weak one; scene alpha is the play-plane mask. Spaghettification injects into MeshStandardMaterial (lighting and flat shading for free), stretches in world space after the instance transform and adds a spiral curl and glow, because a straight stick read as a stretched rock. The disk runs on a "heat" level (growth plus recent meals) and its turbulence is a cached polar map (the disk was half the phone frame). One quality ladder serves the auto scaler and the presets; the scaler never retries a failed level and gives up when lower quality does not speed frames up. Audio is event driven through small hooks so game logic never imports audio; the AudioContext is made lazily on the first gesture. Menus are plain DOM overlays driven by a three-state `mode`. Caddy (not nginx) for brotli from precompressed files; the image reaches TrueNAS by `docker save` and `load` (no git remote). Share card: Web Share only on touch-first devices; the photo is the biggest meal's moment.
